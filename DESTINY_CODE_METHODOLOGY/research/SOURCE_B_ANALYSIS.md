# Independent analysis: Source B

## Contribution and dependence

`Дополнительные ключи`, Albina Matrix, 2022, describes itself as updated part 2. B:6 says the main algorithm is in part 1; B:8 starts after the personal and ancestral squares. B is strongest as a supplement: talent/inspiration meanings, material-pattern meanings and detailed channel commentary. It is not independent confirmation of A's entire numerical foundation.

## Mathematical system actually documented

The numeric chart in B has personal center 8 and cardinal values 5,1,7,13. Tail inner = R22(13+8)=21 and middle = R22(13+21)=7 (B:9–10). Talent inner = 1+8=9 and middle = 1+9=10; physical top-down order is 1–10–9 (B:12–16). Money entry = 7+8=15; connector = R22(21+15)=9; relationship midpoint = R22(21+9)=3; money midpoint = R22(15+9)=6 (B:17–18). Material middle = 7+15=22 (B:19). Parent inner = 5+8=13 and middle = 5+13=18 (B:20, verified visually).

For ancestral branches, B:21 explicitly adds the **personal center 8** to each corner: NW 6→14→20; NE 8→16→6; SE 20→10→3; SW 18→8→8. This directly conflicts with C's use of a distinct family aggregate. The conflict is arithmetic, not merely a different translation of the same position.

## Interpretation roles and tensions

B:12 gives the top arm's physical-order roles as spiritual talent, intellect, and self-expression. B:22 instead describes the middle as a divine talent and the third as past-life skill. C:28–29 associates the inner point with self-expression and the middle with inherited/past skill. The arithmetic is shared, but these fine-grained labels are not interchangeable. The proposed model uses C's inner/middle roles and records B's alternative vocabulary rather than asserting universal agreement.

B:22 also suggests considering talents together with the financial channel. This supports a domain-composition idea, but not a promise of high income. B:24–26 relates inspiration to each month archetype and extends the discussion to all 22 energies. Clinical depression explanations and suggestions about smoking, spending, or political loyalty are not appropriate literal conclusions from a chart. The English adaptations retain initiative, connection, meaningful contribution, imagination and similar symbolic themes without importing those prescriptions.

B:27–41 separates the three parent-channel roles; B:42–49 discusses inherited family themes; B:67–71 describes the relationship midpoint; B:72–88 describes resource/work themes; B:89–94 gives material-pattern guidance. These domain distinctions are more useful for a reading engine than repeating the general appendix for every position.

## Diagram and order issues

B:16,18,23,90 are diagram-dominant and OCR alone yields almost no text. Visual review shows that B:18 explicitly labels relationships as 21–3–9 and finance as 15–6–9 even though the icon placement can mislead a reader. Channel identity must come from its formula and role, not a heart/dollar symbol. B:20's first arithmetic line was omitted in OCR but is visible in the scan. These are specific limitations of text-only processing, not new conventions.

B's tail listing and A's listing share much material. Triple orientation must be preserved; no source-supported universal permutation rule was located. B:6 mentions four purposes, including planetary purpose, yet the additional construction supplies no reproducible planetary formula. This is an unsupported implementation candidate, not grounds to invent one.

## Independent verdict

B reinforces the personal-center ancestry convention through an explicit worked chart and adds valuable topic-specific semantics. Its archetype appendix is not identical to A's: B gives more direct advice, while A often gives longer general descriptions. Their shared author and overlapping catalog reduce their evidentiary independence. The separate varna material remains outside the chosen matrix domain.
