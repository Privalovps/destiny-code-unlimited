## Interpretation evidence

All 22 archetypes have stable IDs and source ranges in arcana.json. All English sentences are original editorial synthesis of general archetype themes. A range establishes the general concept, not a precise quotation corresponding to every domain sentence. Family and karmic fields deliberately reformulate literal source claims into symbolic possibilities.
The 26 C-tail keys have exact numeric tuples and page ranges in karmic_patterns.json. Their English titles and practices are editorial. The generic-code catalog preserves printed heading values and heading pages but does not activate their narratives.
All composition rules carry their own classification. COM-REPEAT and COM-CONTRAST are original editorial rules without a claimed source algorithm. Source-inspired channel composition cites the source channel concepts but does not pretend the new English algorithm was printed in a book.
Report organization, chapter count, access policy, technical schemas and migration choices are product/engineering synthesis after the research. The existing app is not an authority for any formula.

## Coverage evidence

PAGE_COVERAGE.csv includes630 rows, processing mode, extracted character count, text digest, section and review mode. Indexed/section-screened is distinct from directly inspected text; visual review is separate. Machine extraction of a page is not counted as an exhaustive semantic read. The package does not redistribute book OCR, scanned pages or illustrations.
