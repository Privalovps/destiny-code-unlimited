# Independent analysis: Source C

## Contribution

Maria Priemykhova's AST 2025 volume is a self-contained construction and interpretation system with its own declared authorial treatment. It supplies the clearest complete chain for an expanded single-date matrix among the reviewed methodological material. That completeness motivates the proposed selection; publication date and number of pages do not make a source more true.

## Arithmetic and zones

C:8 gives the 22-range digit-sum reduction. C:9–18 builds the day/month/year/lower diamond, personal center and four ancestral corners. C:20–21 introduces a separate family aggregate, the reduced sum of all four corners. C:33–37 uses this **family aggregate** to calculate the four inner ancestral branches. It does not substitute the personal center.

The four cardinal inner/middle arms still use the personal center: tail C:21–22; talents C:28–29; material C:29–30; parent C:31–32. C:38–39 adds heart-desire points between the center and left/top inner nodes. C:39–42 calculates the five-node wellbeing path comprising bottom entry, love midpoint, shared connector, money midpoint, and right entry. The overlapping three-node channels are not five independent observations.

C:23–27 supplies Earth/Sky and paternal/maternal intermediate aggregates, then personal/social/spiritual purposes. This amounts to 30 diagram nodes plus one family aggregate and seven line/purpose aggregates in the proposed catalog. The coordinates in our data are a new layout convention; the underlying orientation comes from C's diagrams.

## Position-sensitive meanings

C:44 explains that its detailed archetype treatment distinguishes day, month, year and lower-point contexts; the following archetype sections run through C:138. The day is a portrait; the month is inspiration/spiritual connection; the year is material learning; the lower point is a main lesson. The top-inner point is self-expression and the top-middle point is skills in C:28–29. The money midpoint is an occupational theme, the love midpoint a partner-quality theme, and the connector concerns both spheres. These roles should survive any change in English chapter names.

The source presents reincarnation, supernatural support, causal financial/health effects and family inheritance as literal explanations. The proposed English framework declares an editorial change: it uses the same symbolic positions to examine possibilities, without representing such claims as established facts. A date cannot determine a current positive/negative state. A source reference establishes the origin of an idea, not its empirical validity.

## Code reading

C:140–141 states that location matters and describes a root-first calculation-order reading; the example includes 9–19–10, 21–5–8 and 12–22–7. C:226 unambiguously defines the tail example as 5–15–20, with bottom first, then inner, then middle. The 26 tail headings include separate 18–9–9, 9–9–18 and 9–18–9 entries (C:332,336,341). These cannot be merged just because their multisets match.

The general-code chapter is harder to automate. For example, heading 3–17–14 (C:141) does not satisfy R22(first+second)=third; it does satisfy R22(first+third)=second. Other printed headings have different relations or could refer to different triplets. The book provides narrative programs, but a universal eligible-triplet detector is not unambiguously established in the reviewed rules. All extracted headings are inventoried with page references; automatic matching is disabled. That preserves the evidence instead of inventing a detector.

## Source-specific limitations

C:21 uses a different enumeration of the tail values in prose; C:226's explicit calculation-order instruction takes precedence for the named-tail lookup. C:41 gives a 60% compatibility assertion without a reproducible statistical basis in the supplied construction. It is not an output probability. Heart points are symbolic chakra associations, not measurements of an organ. The appendix C:348–351 is a compact cross-check, not a complete substitute for the position-sensitive chapter.

## Independent verdict

Select the complete C calculation chain as the proposed baseline, retain A/B ancestry as a distinct reference alternative, and label all new inclusive English wording as editorial synthesis. Do not activate the ambiguous generic-program detector. The numerical baseline is source-supported and testable; the books do not supply scientific validation.
