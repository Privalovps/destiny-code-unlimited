# Proposed coherent methodology

**Research identifier:** `dc-symbolic-1.0.0-proposed`.
**Selected calculation profile:** `C-family-center-v1`.
**Working framework name:** Role-Based 22-Archetype Reflection.

This is an original editorial framework built on a selected source calculation chain. It is not presented as a new historically established school or an official version of any author's course. The name describes the reading method: each number is interpreted through its specific position and declared channel. It is independent of an application, a report chapter count, or an existing chart.

## Three layers

1. **Calculation evidence.** C:8–42 defines the selected arithmetic; shared A/B formulas are additional witnesses. Every value has inputs, reduction steps and page references. An alternative A/B ancestry convention is retained under a different profile and is never partially blended into C.
2. **Symbolic vocabulary.** The 22 archetypes preserve the source numbering and major distinctions. A symbol supports a theme to consider, not a measurement of personality, health, wealth or fate.
3. **Editorial composition.** The English prose, practical examples, neutral family framing, recurrence explanations, contrast rules, and report ordering are original. They are tagged `editorial_synthesis` even when their underlying vocabulary is sourced.

## Mathematical extent

The catalog contains 30 on-chart nodes: the nine primary nodes; eight cardinal arm points; eight ancestral arm points; two heart-desire points; and three wellbeing-path points. Add one detached family aggregate and seven purpose/line aggregates for **38 computed values**. A number of values are shared between channels. Reusing one node in two channels does not create an additional independent position.

The three purpose results are personal, social and spiritual. The four intermediate line aggregates are Earth, Sky, paternal and maternal. The family aggregate is numerically and conceptually separate from the personal center and from social purpose, even when two of them happen to coincide in a sample.

## Interpretation procedure

- Establish the core and day themes, distinguishing an organizing preference from visible expression.
- Read talents through the month arm. Preserve C's distinction between the inner expression point and the middle skill point.
- Read relationships and resources through their explicit entries, midpoint and shared bridge. Describe a practical tradeoff at the bridge once.
- Read the parent–child arm and each family branch as symbolic roles. Do not infer actual childhood events, hereditary disease, family wrongdoing or relatives' motives.
- Read the bottom triad in calculation order `[bottom,bottom_inner,bottom_mid]`. Use an exact C key only; otherwise identify the bottom archetype as the general learning theme and disclose the unmatched key.
- Explain purpose values from the named intermediate aggregates. Source age bands are background vocabulary, not required life stages.
- Introduce at most two repeated-primary themes and a small number of explicit contrasts. Neither frequency nor position proximity becomes a numeric strength score.
- End with concrete experiments linked to the positions actually discussed. Examples illustrate possible behavior; they are not biography inferred from the date.

An unfamiliar technical term is explained once. An advanced reader can inspect the same position IDs and arithmetic trace used by the accessible prose. The two reading levels never use competing formulas.

## Why C rather than an averaged system

C provides explicit formulas for its additional nodes and a clear tail-order instruction. Selecting its complete construction sequence avoids using A/B's personal-center ancestry formula alongside C's family-center interpretations. A/B are retained for comparison, optional future profiles, and transparent identification of semantic alternatives. No source wins by having newer publication dates or more pages.

## Meaningful boundaries

The matrix does not establish whether someone is currently in a constructive or shadow state. Source tales about reincarnation are handled as the historical vocabulary of the books, not factual claims about a customer. Illness, infertility, trauma, abuse, suicidal behavior, guaranteed wealth, future catastrophe and statistically quantified compatibility are not inferred from a date. This is an explicit editorial departure from some source passages.

Generic C programs remain an evidence catalog until a placement-specific detector can be justified. Their absence does not remove the source's actual formulas or the 26 exact tail keys. Planetary purpose, arbitrary pair tables, forecasts, and caste/varna percentages are not quietly inserted. The varna annex uses a different reduction domain and birth-time assumption.

## Status

The selected numerical baseline is specified and checked against source examples. The English interpretation library is an original editorial proposal. The broader audit processed every page, but exhaustive semantic reading of every narrative entry is not claimed. That distinction remains visible in the coverage log and release decisions. Review of source conflicts and broader narrative coverage must not be hidden by calling the whole package historically authoritative or fully production-approved.
