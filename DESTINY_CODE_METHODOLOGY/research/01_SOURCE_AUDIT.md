# Source audit and research scope

This research was organized around the books, before designing an application handoff. No existing chart, formula, chapter count, or report text was used as evidence. The source base consists of two publications by Albina Matrix and one by Maria Priemykhova, not three independent traditions.

## Processing, coverage and limits

All 630 PDF pages were processed and indexed. A and B have no native text layer: their original embedded page images were OCR-processed in Russian and English. The same page is displayed three times across the top of the PDF canvas; the three placements are duplicates, not three different book pages. The lower unused canvas is not missing text. C has a usable native text layer with some control characters and line-break hyphenation.

**This is not a claim that all 630 pages received an exhaustive line-by-line semantic or visual read.** Construction rules, core interpretation passages, catalog structure, source disagreements and selected diagrams were directly inspected; the remainder was extracted and screened at section/index level. `PAGE_COVERAGE.csv` distinguishes these states. `data/coverage_summary.json` reports actual counts. An automatic text extraction is not recorded as a complete human-equivalent reading. No unresolved passage is silently promoted to a verified production rule.

A:32 and B:16,18,23,90 have little or no recognized text because they are diagram-dominant; each was visually inspected. These pages are not empty. The image review recovered B:18's relationship key 21–3–9 and money key 15–6–9. B:20 was visually checked because OCR lost the first arithmetic line. B:21 and C:33 were visually checked to confirm the different ancestry centers. Other non-inspected diagram details remain outside the verified set.

No full OCR transcription, copied book illustration, or substitute paraphrase of the entire books is redistributed in this package. PDF page citations identify where a reviewer can consult the uploaded originals. Book assertions about history, medicine, social hierarchy and causation are treated as assertions in those texts, not as independently verified facts.

## Verified worked examples

A:14–26 and A:33 use 23 January 1987: day 5, month 1, year 7, bottom 13, core 8; purpose 8/16/6. B's additional construction uses the same numeric chart; it is not a third independent fixture. C:9–42 uses 9 December 1991: day 9, month 12, year 20, bottom 5, core 10, family aggregate 11; purpose 10/11/21. All manually captured assertions are in `data/source_example_assertions.json`.

A:111 also gives a varna example for 3 February 1993. This is a different calculation domain: its year digit-root is 4, whereas the 22-range year value is 22. Its 40/10/10/40 weights and before-01:30 previous-date exception are inventoried and excluded from the matrix engine. Do not mistake them for missing matrix rules.
