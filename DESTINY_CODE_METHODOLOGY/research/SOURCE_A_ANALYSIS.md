# Independent analysis: Source A

## Contribution

Albina Matrix's 2022 `От А до Я` is a broad introduction with a worked chart, purpose arithmetic, topic-specific readings and a general archetype appendix. Its narrative assumes reincarnation and inherited family tasks; those assumptions explain the author's symbolism but do not provide empirical evidence about an individual.

## Mathematical system

A:11 and A:14–17 preserve numbers up to 22 and digit-sum higher values. Birth day is reduced if needed, month is retained, year digits are added and reduced. The lower point is the reduced sum of those three values; the personal center is the reduced sum of all four. Neighboring outer points create the four ancestral corners (A:18). Purpose uses separately reduced opposing endpoints and then their aggregates (A:21,33).

The four cardinal arms add their outer value to the personal center for the inner value, then add the outer and inner values for the middle point (A:22–25). The ancestral arms also use the **personal center** (A:26). That is the defining ancestry convention of this source. The money/relationship connector is the reduced sum of the right and bottom inner points; each domain midpoint adds its entry to that connector (A:24).

## Meaning and hierarchy

A:28 identifies the personal center as an organizing quality, the bottom triad as recurring lessons, the top triad as talents/inspiration, the day as the outward portrait, the left triad as parent–child work, and the right triad as material patterns. A:29 qualifies the source's positive/negative tendencies by chart region. This is a reading heuristic, not an observable state that a date can determine. The text recommends different starting emphases: A:28 first discusses the center, while A:68 recommends starting with the karmic tail. There is no objectively specified priority weight to implement.

A:43–44 specifies the parent–child roles in **physical order**: outer day = reason/theme of the parent relationship; middle = relationship task; inner = mistakes in relationships with parents or children. That order differs from the calculation sequence outer→inner→middle. A:89 makes the relationship midpoint a partner-quality theme and the connector a resource/relationship interaction. A:93 and 99 make the resource midpoint a work theme, with the source also giving prescriptive spending suggestions. These suggestions are not financial guarantees.

A's appendix (117–143) consistently places Justice at 8 and Strength at 11. Its archetypes emphasize practical social roles: teaching for 5, directed action for 7, expertise for 9, service for 12, change for 13, public creative expression for 17, kinship for 20 and breadth for 21. The new English library preserves these distinctions while replacing gender prescriptions and fatalistic claims with explicitly original reflective wording.

## Named combinations and limitations

A:21–22 calculates the 1987 tail as bottom/middle/inner = 13–7–21. Its named catalog includes 21/7/13 under a destructive historical label (A:84). This is not permission to sort all triples: the catalog's orientation must be reconciled explicitly before using its named labels. The three 9/9/18 variants are grouped together in A:69, whereas C gives three separate entries. A does not support a universal pairwise compatibility table, numeric repetition multiplier, deterministic relationship prediction, or a tested probability of professional success.

A:28 calls the parent channel right and the material channel left in prose, contrary to A:25 and the diagrams. The formula and visual positions resolve the location; do not swap the nodes. Some negative descriptions assert illness or past misconduct from symbols. Those are source beliefs and are not transferred as factual user claims.

## Separate annex

A:109–115 describes castes/varnas. A:111 computes ordinary digit roots of day, month, year and their total, weights them 40%,10%,10%,40%, and adds an expression number from day plus month. It introduces a time-of-birth exception before 01:30. This annex uses a different numerical domain and social hierarchy. It is documented as a source-specific module, not blended into the 22-archetype chart.

## Independent verdict

A supports a coherent personal-center ancestry profile and rich domain interpretation. It does not justify overriding C's different family-center profile, nor does its overlap with B provide an independent vote. It remains useful as a comparative witness and for broad archetypal distinctions. Uninspected individual passages remain identified through the page coverage log.
