## Selection rationale

The recommended numerical baseline is C's full chain, not a majority vote or a blend of convenient formulas. C explicitly supplies its own personal center, family aggregate, ancestry arms, desire points, and three purpose computations in one construction sequence. A and B remain valuable comparative witnesses and support a separate ancestry profile. Their common author and repeated worked example must not be counted as two independent validations.

The choice concerns internal coherence in an esoteric system. It establishes no external proof that either tradition predicts people or events. The content framework keeps sourced mathematics separate from symbolic vocabulary and from newly written English prose. Generic-code matching, planetary purpose and medical/compatibility scoring remain outside the verified baseline.
