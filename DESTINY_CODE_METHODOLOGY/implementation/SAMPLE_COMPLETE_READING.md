## How to read this matrix

An archetype is a symbolic pattern associated with one of 22 numbered themes. A position gives that theme a role; a channel joins specified positions. Constructive and shadow readings are alternative possibilities to consider, not states diagnosed from your birthday. This reading uses the C-family-center convention and original English editorial interpretations.

Your date produces a diagram with 30 named nodes, a separate family-resource aggregate, and seven line/purpose aggregates. Repeated values arise partly because the numbers are calculated from one another. They are not independent evidence about you.

## Core patterns and outward expression

The center describes an organizing preference; the birth-day point describes outward expression. The two roles can show different ways to approach the same situation.

Personal center [core] = 10 — The Wheel of Fortune. The organizing preference used to connect the reading. The organizing symbolic theme is cycles and responsive opportunity. Notice changing conditions, cooperate with available openings, and adjust without losing your direction. For example: A project changes direction, and you revise the plan while keeping the original delivery promise visible. 

Birth-day portrait [day] = 9 — The Hermit. How the archetype can appear in ordinary self-expression. Study patiently, form a considered view, and offer knowledge without needing constant attention. 

5 (learning and transmission) occurs at distinct primary nodes bottom, ne. The role changes with each position. This is structural recurrence, not a multiplier for a measured trait.

A shadow possibility to examine: Waiting for luck to do the work, changing plans at every fluctuation, or avoiding commitments in pursuit of ease. This is a contrast to test against experience, not a conclusion about your current behavior.

## Talents, skills, and expression

The top arm separates inspiration, expression, and skills. These are practices to explore in combination, rather than a list of abilities your birthday certifies.

Month / spiritual inspiration [month] = 12 — The Hanged Man. What may feel inspiring or worth practicing. Reframing, creative interpretation, careful support, and service design. This describes a practice to explore, not a certified ability. For example: You help a friend for an agreed hour rather than silently canceling your own commitments. 

Talent: self-expression [month_inner] = 22 — The Fool. How a skill or idea can be expressed and communicated. Improvisation, experimentation, play, and generating fresh alternatives. This describes a practice to explore, not a certified ability. 

Talent: skills and practice [month_mid] = 7 — The Chariot. A skill pattern to develop through practice. Execution, logistics, motivation, and coordinating action. This describes a practice to explore, not a certified ability. 

Work experiment: combine improvisation, experimentation, play, and generating fresh alternatives with the breadth and integration theme at money_center=21. Test it in a small useful deliverable before deciding whether it suits you.

A shadow possibility to examine: Making yourself indispensable through sacrifice, staying indefinitely suspended, or expecting gratitude for unrequested help. This is a contrast to test against experience, not a conclusion about your current behavior.

## Love and reciprocal partnership

This channel connects an entry theme, qualities valued in partnership, and a shared point where relationships and resources meet.

Relationship-channel entry [bottom_inner] = 15 — The Devil. The learning theme carried into reciprocal partnership. Attraction benefits from explicit consent and freedom to say no. For example: When a shared plan changes, state what you want directly and ask the other person what they want, while leaving room for either person to say no. 

Relationship-channel midpoint [love_center] = 6 — The Lovers. Qualities valued in reciprocal partnership. Choose reciprocal connection over performing an ideal version of yourself. 

Shared relationship–resource bridge [bridge] = 18 — The Moon. How relationship commitments and resource commitments affect one another. Consider the theme of imagination and uncertainty when negotiating both time and resources. Ask for clarity before turning an ambiguous event into a relationship narrative. 

A practical bridge exercise is to compare the facts of a shared commitment with the assumptions each person brought to it: what time or money was actually agreed, and what did each person only expect?

A shadow possibility to examine: Letting approval decide for you, idealizing a partner, or postponing a choice to avoid disappointment. This is a contrast to test against experience, not a conclusion about your current behavior.

## Money, work, and material habits

The right arm describes material habits; the money midpoint adds a work theme. Keeping these roles separate is more useful than treating every number here as a career prediction.

Year / material learning [year] = 20 — Judgment. How resources and material responsibilities can be approached. Shared knowledge can be a resource; a family connection does not prove a business is suitable. For example: You preserve a useful family practice while changing a rule that no longer fits your circumstances. 

Money-channel entry [year_inner] = 3 — The Empress. The entry theme linking material habits to present resource choices. Comfort and quality can be worthwhile uses of money; status is a poor substitute for deciding what is enough. 

Material use and spending theme [year_mid] = 5 — The Hierophant. How resources can be used and what priorities shape spending. Knowledge can become a useful service when it solves a real problem; authority alone does not establish value. 

Money-channel midpoint [money_center] = 21 — The World. Work settings and skills associated with a resource theme. A larger market or network creates complexity as well as possibilities; the chart does not validate expansion. 

The shared bridge is again 18 (The Moon). Use the same relationship–resource interpretation described in the relationships chapter, now when agreeing on work time, spending, or shared responsibilities.

A shadow possibility to examine: Expanding before finishing, dismissing local needs as too small, or mistaking broad interest for a workable plan. This is a contrast to test against experience, not a conclusion about your current behavior.

Heart desire, Earth axis [heart_earth] = 11 — Strength. What feels meaningful in daily material life. A meaningful outlet for determination and enough room to recover. 

A shadow possibility to examine: Withdrawing before others can know you, using expertise to dismiss them, or waiting for complete knowledge before contributing. This is a contrast to test against experience, not a conclusion about your current behavior.

## Recurring lessons and the ordered tail

The ordered learning key is 5–15–20: bottom, inner, then middle. The physical bottom-to-center order is 5–20–15; it is not the catalog lookup order.

Knowledge, influence, and belonging. Consider whether teaching becomes a way to control approval or defend a family expectation. Share what you know while allowing a listener to disagree. This is an original present-life reflection on a source-specific key, not a statement that you performed a particular act in a past life.

Main learning point [bottom] = 5 — The Hierophant. A recurring learning theme in the source tradition. Update one belief when evidence or experience challenges it. 

The supporting themes are desire and the use of power and inheritance and renewal. Interpret these in the learning channel; do not borrow a partner or work profile simply because the same numbers occur elsewhere.

## Purpose, values, and inner priorities

Earth=11; Sky=17; personal purpose=10. Paternal=10; maternal=19; social purpose=11. Spiritual purpose=21 combines the two purposes. Each named intermediate value is reduced before the next formula.

Personal purpose [purpose_personal] = 10 — The Wheel of Fortune. Integration of inner and material development. Combine a flexible route with a stable minimum commitment. Adaptation, connecting opportunities, and responding to a changing environment. 

Social purpose [purpose_social] = 11 — Strength. Contribution and exchange with other people. Practice restraint as deliberately as effort. Persistence, demanding execution, motivation, and steady effort. 

Spiritual purpose [purpose_spiritual] = 21 — The World. Integration of personal and social purpose. Complete one local commitment before extending the scope. Cross-cultural communication, systems thinking, and integrating multiple viewpoints. 

Heart desire, Earth axis [heart_earth] = 11 — Strength. What feels meaningful in daily material life. The capacity and measured force theme appears again in a different role. 

Heart desire, Sky axis [heart_sky] = 5 — The Hierophant. What feels meaningful in inspiration and inner life. A meaningful practice and a community that takes learning seriously. 

The source emphasizes personal development around 20–40 and contribution around 40–60 while treating spiritual purpose as lifelong. These are symbolic emphases, not deadlines. A different life sequence does not indicate failure.
