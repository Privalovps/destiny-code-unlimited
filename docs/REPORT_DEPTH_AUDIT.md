# Expanded reading content audit — November 18, 2026

## Cause and architecture change

The previous composer chose one short domain sentence for each position. A global substring de-duplication step exhausted those short alternatives and substituted placeholder role language. Constructive/shadow coverage was attached only to a chapter anchor; family branches mostly listed meanings. Per-position calculation details repeated the same methodological explanation and inflated the earlier body/detail count.

The new content edition is `en-US-role-editorial-2.0.0`. Method `dc-symbolic-1.0.0-proposed`, profile `C-family-center-v1`, all 38 values, numbering and source-data hash are unchanged. The package remains byte-identical. No general program matcher is enabled.

`depth-library.ts` adds 22 original English profiles with distinct principles, resources, excesses, discriminations and experiments, plus concise constructive/pressure application cues. `depth.ts` adds 38 explicit role contexts, role-specific review questions and situations. An archetype's full explanation appears once; repeated appearances use the actual role/domain and application cues. Sentence-level suppression removes repeated source statements without replacing them with fallback filler. The talent, relationship, material, family and four ancestral channels use only the approved declared sequences. Core/portrait, purpose and integration readings retain their actual node references. These are editorial syntheses of documented roles, not newly claimed traditional combination laws.

## Three actual expanded examples (fixed 1991-12-09)

### core

At the center, this theme describes the organizing preference you return to when several demands compete. It is useful to compare what feels internally coherent with the way you actually make a decision, rather than trying to make every part of your life resemble one symbol. The Wheel of Fortune attends to cycles and changing conditions. Its focus is responsive timing: noticing what is available now without treating an opening as a guarantee or a changing situation as a command.  Notice what you preserve when you have to change your approach. That priority reveals more about this reading than how strongly you identify with the archetype’s name. Adaptation can preserve a worthwhile aim while the route changes. An opportunity becomes useful through a decision and follow-through, not through its attractiveness alone. Flexibility can turn into drift when every fluctuation produces a new plan. Waiting for ease or luck may avoid the repetitive effort that allows an opening to become a result. Distinguish a change in conditions from a loss of interest. A stable minimum commitment helps you revise the method without abandoning the original purpose unnecessarily. Picture a decision in which two worthwhile commitments require different responses. Choose what will remain consistent through one changing situation and what you are willing to adjust, then review both after a defined interval.

The distinction in your reading is between 10 / The Wheel of Fortune at the center and 9 / The Hermit in the portrait. The center asks what makes a choice feel coherent from within; the portrait asks how that choice becomes understandable to someone meeting you. An emphasis on cycles and responsive opportunity can guide an intention while depth and independent understanding shapes the way it is offered. Neither is a mask to remove; they address different parts of participation. Imagine being asked to contribute to a new project. What you need to preserve internally may not be obvious in the first thing you say. Explain the priority as well as the proposed action. Constructively, the portrait makes the central preference accessible. A shadow tension appears if a convenient outward response commits you to something the center does not support, or if private certainty replaces making yourself understandable. Compare the two roles through one recent decision: what mattered privately, what was actually communicated and what the other person could reasonably have understood. That comparison adds information that neither isolated archetype can supply.

### relationships

The relationship entry applies the inner learning theme to reciprocity. It concerns the response you bring when another person has needs and preferences of their own, rather than the explanation of the full learning pattern later in this reading. The Devil makes desire, incentive and power available for honest examination. Attraction and ambition are not treated as moral defects; the question is what terms a desired result asks you to accept. Attraction benefits from explicit consent and freedom to say no. Notice the step between feeling a familiar tension and asking another person to resolve it. That is where a more deliberate response can enter. Recognizing an incentive can support clear negotiation. Enjoyment becomes more deliberate when you can acknowledge its appeal without surrendering the freedom to refuse. A reward can begin to dictate the decision. Persuasion becomes coercive when approval, access or affection is used to make a genuine refusal costly. Wanting something strongly is different from being obliged to pursue it. Consider the conditions you would reject even if the offered reward remained attractive. Picture preparing to discuss a concern without making the other person responsible for an unspoken expectation. Name both the desired benefit and the unacceptable cost before entering a negotiation, and check that everyone retains a meaningful choice.

The relationship channel is read as entry → shared bridge → partnership midpoint. It describes a negotiation among roles, not a sequence of predicted romantic events. In this order, relationship-channel entry brings 15, The Devil, with its emphasis on desire and the use of power; shared relationship–resource bridge brings 18, The Moon, with its emphasis on imagination and uncertainty; relationship-channel midpoint brings 6, The Lovers, with its emphasis on connection and deliberate choice. The entry brings desire and the use of power into reciprocity. The bridge adds imagination and uncertainty to the practical agreements that support it, while connection and deliberate choice describes qualities worth discussing in partnership. A constructive starting action is to name the desired reward and preserve the freedom to refuse its terms. The next role asks you to separate the observed detail from the story built around it. If entry becomes an automatic response, the bridge can remain an unspoken assumption. The valued quality at the midpoint may then be expected as proof of affection rather than negotiated as a shared practice. The specific pressure to watch across these two roles is allowing approval or access to set the conditions of consent alongside letting a compelling interpretation settle an unanswered question. For example, one person wants a different pattern of contact during a demanding work period. Begin with name the incentive or power imbalance instead of using it covertly. Then make the actual time or resource constraint explicit. Finally ask how the partnership quality represented by The Lovers could be expressed within that constraint. Attraction, emotional response and mutual expectation need not arrive in the same form. A constructive agreement names what each person offers, what each person needs and when the arrangement can be reviewed. No component establishes what another person feels.

### resources

The material anchor concerns the approach brought to resources and responsibility. Money is one expression; equipment, working time and obligations also matter. The relevant question is how something useful is maintained, allocated or changed. Judgment connects inheritance with renewal. Family, community and collective memory provide material to understand and choose from, rather than a finished script that must be repeated. Shared knowledge can be a resource; a family connection does not prove a business is suitable. Distinguish a resource that supports a defined aim from one kept mainly for reassurance or appearance. The same object or expense can serve either function. Connecting generations can preserve knowledge while making it usable in a changed setting. Belonging can support independent judgment instead of replacing it. Loyalty may become obedience, or a fixed story may assign blame before anyone speaks. Trying to repair everyone’s history can obscure the responsibilities that actually belong to you. Continuing a tradition is a present choice. It is possible to value its intention and revise its form without demanding that every relative agree. Picture deciding which commitments a limited amount of time and money can realistically support. Name one inherited practice you value, the reason it matters and one adjustment that would let you choose it more freely today.

The material triad starts with an approach to responsibility, develops it through entry practice and then examines resource use. In this order, year / material learning brings 20, Judgment, with its emphasis on inheritance and renewal; money-channel entry brings 3, The Empress, with its emphasis on cultivation and tangible care; material use and spending theme brings 5, The Hierophant, with its emphasis on learning and transmission. inheritance and renewal is the starting perspective; cultivation and tangible care asks how it enters a present commitment; learning and transmission brings attention to what is actually prioritized afterward. A constructive starting action is to choose what to carry forward and explain what you would revise. The next role asks you to provide the form of practical care the recipient actually wants. A practical difficulty can arise when an attractive aim is accepted before the recurring cost is understood. The reverse is also possible: protecting an existing allocation may prevent a useful revision. The specific pressure to watch across these two roles is treating a different present choice as rejection of belonging alongside turning the effort invested into a claim on the recipient. For example, consider a project that requires a new recurring expense. Separate the purpose of the project, the conditions for accepting the expense and the commitments that would have to change to sustain it. The useful result is a clearer allocation, not an optimistic forecast. Assess the actual resources and alternatives independently of the symbolic reading.
